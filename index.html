How to play

  1. Choose Manual, then click a tower to lift its top disk.
  2. Click another tower to drop it there. Click the same tower again to put it back.
  3. Only the top disk moves, and a larger disk never sits on a smaller one.
  4. Choose Automatic and press Start to watch Prolog solve it.

How the recursion works

To move N disks from A to C: move N−1 disks to B, move the largest disk to C, then move the N−1 disks from B onto C. The smallest case, zero disks, needs no moves. The result is always 2N − 1 moves.

hanoi(0, _, _, _, []) :- !.
hanoi(N, From, To, Via, Moves) :-
    N1 is N - 1,
    hanoi(N1, From, Via, To, Before),
    hanoi(N1, Via, To, From, After),
    append(Before, [move(N,From,To)], Mid),
    append(Mid, After, Moves).
